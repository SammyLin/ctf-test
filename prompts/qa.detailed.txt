Goal

Questions

2. In what ways can an attribute be marked private, where is that decided, how is the result cached, and where are private attributes removed from API output?
3. For the users-permissions rate limiter: how is the rate-limit key built, on which routes is the request body's email left out of the key and why, and what are the default limits?
